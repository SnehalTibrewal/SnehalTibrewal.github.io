I’m a physics PhD student at the University of Texas at Austin, working with Prof. Deirdre Shoemaker. I’m interested in decoding information about orbital dynamics from gravitational waves emitted by the collision of compact objects like black holes or neutron stars.

The orbit is an eccentric inspiral in general relativity: it precesses every lap and shrinks as it radiates gravitational waves, until merger. Effects exaggerated.
